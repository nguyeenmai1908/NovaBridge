The hidden costs

Avoidance doesn't stop the conversation. It just delays it.

Each of these looks minor from where you sit. Open one to see what it's actually costing.

Teammates quietly cover the gap

The work still ships, so nothing looks broken. The cost lands on the people absorbing it.

The team pays
Problems stop reaching you

People stop raising issues with you. What does reach you arrives late, when it's expensive to fix.

You pay
Good people start looking

The ones carrying the extra load update their LinkedIn. You find out when they resign.

What does one avoided conversation cost?

Researchers asked employees what one avoided conversation costs their organization. What was the average estimate?

$7,500

and more than seven workdays

Not every terminated employee. Not every resignation.

Every. Avoided. Conversation.

Source: VitalSmarts (now Crucial Learning), Costly Conversations study, 2016.
