Before you go in.

Five questions to answer before any performance conversation. You don't need to memorize this. Open it, answer it, go in.

Have someone specific in mind? Use their real situation. Still building the skill? Pick a past conversation or imagine a realistic one — the practice works either way.

0 of 5 ready You're ready. Go in.
O
What specific behavior did I observe?

Not a feeling. Not a judgment. Something a camera could have recorded.

"I noticed _______ on _______."

If you can't complete this sentence — you're not ready yet. Gather more information first.

N
What did that behavior cost?

Not how it made you feel. What did it cost the team, the client, the business?

"When this happens, it means _______."

If your answer is a feeling — keep going. Find the consequence underneath.

A
What question haven't I asked yet?

Before you walk in with a solution — write the question you'll ask first.

"Is there something _______ I'm not aware of?"

Do not skip this. Whatever you think the answer is — ask anyway.

A
What will we each do next?

Draft your close before you go in.

"I'll _______ by _______.
Can you _______ by _______?"

Named person. Named action. Named date. Both sides. Both accountable.

?
What am I assuming that might be wrong?

This question isn't in any framework. It's the one that makes all four steps above work.

Be willing to discover you're wrong about it.
