What you'll be able to do
  • Spot when a delay is a threat response rather than a character flaw, and why that instinct starts costing you once you manage people.
  • Tell which of three reasons is driving a manager's avoidance.
  • Name the costs that build up unseen: on the team, on you, and on the business.
  • Recognize which avoidance pattern is closest to your own.
